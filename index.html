Personal / Private League Application

Fantasy Football Intelligence Engine

A personal fantasy football analytics and decision-support application designed to synchronize a private Yahoo Fantasy Football league with a local intelligence and analysis system.

Purpose

The application combines Yahoo Fantasy league state with independently sourced NFL information to help analyze roster decisions throughout the fantasy season. It is intended primarily for one private 12-team fantasy football league.

Yahoo Fantasy data used

League stateLeague settings, metadata and current week.
Teams & rostersTeam identities, managers, current rosters and weekly rosters.
TransactionsAdds, drops, waivers and trades for league-state tracking.
MatchupsWeekly opponents, team scores and matchup results.
StandingsCurrent wins, losses, ties and standings information.
PlayersYahoo player identifiers, ownership status and fantasy-relevant player data.

Intended use

Yahoo Fantasy data is used to maintain an accurate local representation of the private league so that roster, waiver, matchup, injury-impact, trade and player opportunity analysis can be performed consistently over the course of the season.

The application is designed as a read-only analytics tool. It does not place fantasy transactions, modify rosters, change league settings, or otherwise write changes to Yahoo.

Additional NFL analysis

The application may combine league information with independently sourced NFL performance, usage, injury, depth-chart, schedule, matchup and other football information to evaluate changes in player opportunity and fantasy value.
